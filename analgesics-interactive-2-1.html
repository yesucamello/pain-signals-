Analgesics lab: you control the signal

Pick a patient, send a pain signal, then give drugs and watch where each one acts and what it costs. Nothing moves until you tell it to.

Tap the i badges in the picture to learn what happens at each spot.
How to play1) Send a pain signal. 2) Give a drug and send it again. 3) Watch the monitors. Try each patient with each drug class.

Acts mainly in the CNS. 1,000 mg per dose. Doses given: 0
